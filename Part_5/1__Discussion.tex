This part reflects on the main findings of the thesis from a broader perspective by focusing on what the results imply for data-driven planning and operations in \acp{bss}, which assumptions and data constraints shape their applicability, and how the proposed approaches can inform practice.

This part comprises a Discussion and a Conclusions chapter (Chapters~\ref{ch:discussion} and~\ref{ch:conclusions}, respectively). The Discussion chapter is organized around the three main contributions: station planning (Section~\ref{sec:disc_stations}), modeling fleet mobility and battery status (Section~\ref{sec:disc_battery}), and component failure forecasting (Section~\ref{sec:disc_maintenance}). It concludes by synthesizing the shared insights that emerge across these contributions (Section~\ref{sec:disc_shared_insights}). Chapter~\ref{ch:conclusions} then summarizes the overall contributions and key findings of the thesis.
