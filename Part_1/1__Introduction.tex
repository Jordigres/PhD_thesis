\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:intro}

Urban growth and environmental pressure have made sustainable mobility a policy priority, and digital data together with \ac{ai} have expanded what can be analyzed and improved in urban transport. Within this context, an important gap remains in translating these analytical advances into practical methods that improve the planning and operation of docked \acp{bss}. This thesis addresses that gap using Barcelona and its \ac{bss}, \textit{Bicing}, as the principal case, and develops data-driven methods for station network design and system operation. This Chapter begins by presenting the wider context of the     thesis, continues with its motivation and objectives, and ends with the organization of the thesis and an overview of the contributions.
