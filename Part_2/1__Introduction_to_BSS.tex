\chaptermark{Introduction to Bike-Sharing Systems}
\label{ch:intro_bss}

Within this Part~\ref{pt:foundations}, Chapter~\ref{ch:intro_bss} narrows the scope from shared mobility in general to docked \acp{bss} to provide a foundational understanding of these systems. It first situates shared-mobility paradigms, then defines \acp{bss} and their main typologies, and finally traces how these systems have evolved internationally.
