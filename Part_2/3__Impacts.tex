\chapter[Impacts of BSSs]{Impacts of \acp{bss}}
\label{ch:impacts_of_bss}

\acp{bss} are valued not only as an innovative mobility service, but also for their potential impacts. Building on the discussion of users and usage patterns in Chapter~\ref{ch:bss_users_and_their_mobility_patterns}, this Chapter reviews what the literature reports about how these systems affect cities and populations.

The contributions in Parts~\ref{pt:data-driven-station-planning} and~\ref{pt:predictive_operations} develop methods for station planning and predictive operations, rather than directly studying and quantifying these broader impacts; what follows nonetheless clarifies how \acp{bss} interventions are typically evaluated, so that the contributions of this thesis can be interpreted alongside the expectations established in the literature and revisited in Part~\ref{pt:discussion_and_implications}.

Accordingly, this Chapter synthesizes research on the global impacts of \acp{bss} over the past 15 years, organized into five dimensions: mobility and transport, environmental, health and safety, and economic and social impacts.
