\chapter{Closing reflections}
\label{ch:conclusions}

This final chapter synthesizes the contributions developed across the thesis and reflects on their implications for sustainable, data-driven \ac{bss} planning and operations. It closes the narrative initiated in Part~\ref{pt:introduction}, where the context, motivation, and objectives are established, and expanded in Part~\ref{pt:foundations}, where the literature and conceptual background clarify how \acp{bss} are defined, used, evaluated for impacts, and studied with computational methods. Building on that basis, together with the station-planning framework in Part~\ref{pt:data-driven-station-planning} and the predictive operational contributions in Part~\ref{pt:predictive_operations}, this Chapter revisits the research problem and questions, consolidates the main findings, discusses practical and scientific implications, and closes with limitations and directions for future research.
