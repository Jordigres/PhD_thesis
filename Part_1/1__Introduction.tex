\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:intro}

\textcolor{blue}{
    Urban growth and environmental pressure have brought sustainable mobility to the center of policy concern, and digital data together with \ac{ai} have expanded what can be analyzed and improved in urban transport. This thesis takes docked \acp{bss} as its focus and develops data-driven methods for their planning and operation, using Barcelona and \textit{Bicing} as the principal case. This Chapter begins by presenting the wider context of the thesis, continues with its motivation and objectives, and ends with the organization of the thesis and an overview of contributions.
}
