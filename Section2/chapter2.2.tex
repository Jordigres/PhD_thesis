\chapter{Related works}\label{ch:related-works}
The study of emotional state classification is an active and dynamic field of scientific research, encompassing a wide range of publications, including research papers, books, and ongoing projects. Notably, the existing body of literature predominantly focuses on offline processing, while there is comparatively less exploration of online processing. Historically, more emphasis has been placed on single-modal stream processing; however, there is a shift towards utilizing multi-modal approaches. There is a growing interest in real-time emotion classification using multi-modal data streaming scenarios, as single-modal methods are limited in their ability to depict complex emotions accurately. Consequently, the analysis of relevant studies aims to shed light on this evolving area of research.
