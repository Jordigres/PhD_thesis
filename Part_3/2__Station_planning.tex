\chapter[Designing BSS Station Networks]{Designing \ac{bss} Station Networks}
\label{ch:station_planning_in_barcelona}

This Chapter establishes the conceptual and methodological foundations for the station planning framework presented in this Part. It reviews the state of the art in station placement, introduces the optimization problem and the proposed approach and illustrates its use for scenario exploration and incremental network expansion with the Barcelona case study (Chapter~\ref{ch:use_case_barcelona}). The material in this Chapter is adapted from Grau-Escolano et al.~\cite{GrauEscolano2025}, submitted to the \textit{Journal of Urban Mobility} and currently under review.
